Sap Sam Poker

Chinese Poker · 十三張

The classic Chinese Poker game.
Arrange your 13 cards into three poker hands.
Bottom (strongest) ≥ Middle ≥ Top (weakest)

Tip: On iPhone tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen for the full app experience.

Tap cards to select, then tap a row to place them
TOP · FRONT (3 cards) — WEAKEST hand
MIDDLE (5 cards) — SECOND strongest
BOTTOM · BACK (5 cards) — STRONGEST hand
YOUR CARDS (tap to select)
